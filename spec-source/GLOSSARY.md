# Lifecycle Glossary

> Status: Draft

## Accepted Result

The canonical repository result created by an authorized Delivery acceptance
transaction. Its tree is exactly the sealed Candidate tree applied over the
exact admitted parent. The Candidate cannot change the authoritative Atlas
root, so the result contains the admitted Atlas without terminal resampling,
composition, or substitution. The result commit contains no Delivery Control.
Exact Evidence, Founder Decision, transaction facts, and Closure remain in the
sealed Control Record Store archive off HEAD. A reviewer recommendation or
complete Evidence Packet is not an Accepted Result until the transaction
succeeds and is truthfully observed.

## Agent

A probabilistic software worker that receives an Agent Attempt and develops a
proposal in its governed semantic Markdown workspace inside one Execution Cell.
Submission, rather than provider terminal text, freezes the proposed semantics.
An Agent can perform broad labor but has no inherent product, Process, Founder,
Backend, or canonical authority.

## Agent Attempt

One provider-neutral execution contract binding a fresh Founder Brief, role,
exact role subject, Knowledge Projection, exact Candidate Revision Carrier or
other Input Set, capability, Investment, governed semantic-body template,
controlled authoring workspace, compiler profiles, Execution Backend Profile,
Execution Image, and Provider Adapter. The Attempt is frozen before dispatch
and contains no future Work Product, provider observation, Execution Handle, or
provider-authored protocol mechanics.

## Agent Work Product

The immutable Control revision retained from one valid Agent semantic
submission. Its body preserves the Agent-authored Markdown; its typed payload
contains the runtime-normalized semantics and exact Attempt bindings. It is an
Agent proposal, not Candidate observation, Evidence, Founder authority, or
canonical product truth.

## Attempt View

A reducer-generated dossier over one Agent Attempt, optional Agent Work
Product, Execution Receipt, input and optional successor Candidate Revision,
current obligations, and eligible Delivery operations. It is disposable and is
not an independently retained Control family or Execution Cell view.

## Assurance

A governed Knowledge record that owns a non-functional obligation, invariant,
failure limit, operating quality, or risk constraint.

## Atlas

The mandatory external project-context system selected by Lifecycle. Lifecycle
consumes one complete valid normalized Atlas read-only while Atlas retains
ownership of its format, Maps, Areas, Point identities, Resources, Checks,
publication profiles, processing, and validation semantics. Atlas records and
references do not transfer ownership or grant authority.

## Admitted Atlas Snapshot

The complete valid Atlas State, Resolution, normalized model, Resource
bindings, repository commit, and tree selected by an admitted Work Boundary.
It is immutable historical context for that Boundary's Projections, Attempts,
Checks, and Evidence. The active-Delivery branch lease keeps the canonical
checkout and HEAD at that same admitted repository epoch until Closure; a
successor Boundary in the same Delivery cannot adopt another Atlas snapshot.

## Active-Delivery Branch Lease

The exclusive operating claim derived from one applied initial Work Boundary
and active Control state over an exact physical target and canonical branch.
The lease permits at most one admitted, unclosed Delivery for that target
branch. It requires a clean canonical checkout and exact admitted HEAD for all
active nonterminal operations and acceptance. Prepared proposals hold no
lease. The exact acceptance commit is the sole authorized branch motion while
the lease persists through terminal recovery; accepted or no-ship Closure and
Store disposition release it.

## Atlas Resolution

The Lifecycle-owned result binding over one exact Atlas Selection, raw Atlas
State, processor implementation, external resolved Validation Result, and, only
when complete and valid, normalized Atlas model. An invalid or incomplete
resolution contains no normalized-model digest and cannot support Delivery.

## Atlas Selection

The exact supported external release, immutable specification revision,
authored format, processor contract revision, resolved profile, output schemas,
and Lifecycle consumer profile carried by the Repository Contract. Foundation
supports one selection without negotiation or backward compatibility.

## Atlas State

The ordered tracked, non-executable Git blob identity of the repository-root
Atlas consumed by one Lifecycle repository snapshot. It binds bytes and the
fixed entrypoint without interpreting meaning. Atlas Resolution separately
establishes complete valid conformance to the selected Atlas contract.

## Authority

The right to establish or move one class of truth. Authority is typed and
non-transitive: product authority does not imply runtime capability, evidence
does not imply acceptance, and a tool permission does not imply founder
judgment.

## Behavior

A governed Knowledge record that owns a functional product outcome or
externally observable product behavior.

## Blueprint

A governed Knowledge record that owns structural, dependency, boundary,
data-flow, or technical decision meaning.

## Candidate

Delivery's sole reversible development product-state object. It does not exist
merely because initial admission applied. After that effect makes the selected
Work Boundary active, the runtime observes and retains one initialization
Candidate Revision from the immutable product base. The Candidate persists
across productive Agent Attempts, bounded correction, and compatible readmission
through its immutable Candidate Revision lineage and reconstructible Carriers,
not through one physical checkout. It remains noncanonical until an authorized
acceptance transaction makes its exact sealed state canonical over the unchanged
admitted parent. Working and sealed are lifecycle conditions of this same
Candidate, not new record families or identities.

## Candidate Revision

An immutable runtime-observed Control revision identifying one exact valid
Candidate state, its immutable base, state digests, Candidate Revision Carrier,
and predecessor revision when applicable. A builder output creates a successor
only after independent validation and durable Carrier publication. The event
that references it owns the observation context. Receipt, Seal, and acceptance
refer to this exact revision; an unavailable or invalid output creates no
Revision, and Agent or Cell prose cannot manufacture one.

## Candidate Revision Carrier

The immutable content-addressed Git bytes sufficient to reconstruct one exact
Candidate Revision. Its canonical manifest is a digest-bound adjacent Control
file; its complete Git object closure is retained in the installation Carrier
Store while a live or archived Control Store references it. A Carrier contains
no mutable currentness, process authority, materialization path, or Execution
Handle and is not a Control record family.

## Candidate materialization

A disposable filesystem realization reconstructed from one exact Candidate
Revision Carrier for an Agent Attempt, Check, evaluation, or acceptance import.
The primitive materialization contains the exact product files and modes but no
invented commit or `.git` directory. A separately bound immutable repository
context may support a disposable Git view when an operation requires Git
behavior. Either form is execution support, not Candidate identity or
continuity. Writes inside an Attempt Cell are provisional until the runtime
retrieves and validates them, publishes a Carrier, and appends one successor
Candidate Revision.

## Candidate Seal

An immutable runtime-authenticated Control revision selecting the exact
Candidate Revision frozen for one evaluation. A Seal does not create another
Candidate, change Candidate identity, or make Product State canonical. If the
same Candidate returns to correction, the prior Seal remains an immutable
historical evaluation subject and a later evaluation produces a new Seal.

## Capability Profile

The repository-registered semantic ceiling for one class of Agent Attempt,
including product-file writes, subprocesses, product-network classes,
product-credential classes, and external effects. It describes Agent product
capability, not Lifecycle's Docker daemon authority, provider authentication,
provider service channel, or private Backend operations. An Attempt binds the
exact profile and the runtime proves the effective Cell grant is its subset.

## Check Binding

A repository-specific mechanism that obtains evidence for a Check Definition.
It can identify a registered Candidate-relative or Execution-Image-relative
command, inspection, artifact, diff, analysis, or another allowed mechanism.
A host-absolute executable path is not Check identity and cannot be substituted
for the executable selected by the exact Image and Binding.

## Check Definition

A governed Knowledge record that states a falsifiable validation proposition,
its subject, required evidence shape, and limits. It is distinct from an Atlas
Check, which governs Atlas authoring under the external Atlas specification.

## Check Receipt

A runtime-authenticated record of one Check Binding result for one exact
subject, environment, and time. For an allocated Cell it binds the exact
Specification, Backend Profile, Image, Input Set, output, Containment, and
Retirement facts without exposing the private Handle. It distinguishes
Binding-requested conditions, runtime-enforced facts, and Backend or host
assumptions or limitations. A passing receipt is evidence under those recorded
conditions, not acceptance or an unstated isolation guarantee.

## Closure

The immutable terminal Control revision for one Delivery. Closure references
the exact authenticated Founder Decision and applied transaction and records
accepted or no-ship disposition, applicable Boundary and Candidate treatment,
Execution Containment and Retirement, and terminal facts. Completed Execution
Reclamation is not required. Its atomic `closure-recorded` finalization is the
final Journal head and makes Control Record Store sealing eligible. It is
sufficient terminal Process truth but is not Product Knowledge.

An accepted Closure uses Candidate treatment `integrated`: the exact sealed
Candidate tree becomes the accepted result over the exact admitted parent, and
Candidate confinement proves it has no Atlas delta. A no-ship Closure uses
`abandoned` when a Candidate existed and `not-created` when none did.
Abandonment permanently ends Candidate advancement and acceptance without
claiming immediate erasure of every Carrier byte.

## Control

Process-local facts, proposals, judgments, decisions, and effects used to
coordinate a Process and preserve its disposition. Delivery Control is retained
as immutable revisions and append-only events in one Control Record Store.
Control does not own durable product meaning and is not Knowledge.

## Control Record Revision

One immutable retained revision containing stable Process and record identity,
kind, revision number, runtime producer, semantic author and authority,
normalized semantic Markdown, typed operational payload, one-direction typed
relationships, and a logical digest. Currentness and standing are never stored
inside the revision.

## Control Record Event

One immutable member of the closed twenty-two-kind Delivery event sequence.
It binds the exact Delivery, positive contiguous sequence, immediately
preceding event digest except at sequence one, occurrence time, actor, optional
exact Control Record Revision subject, and bounded event-owned payload. An event
owns why and when a fact affected Process reduction; it does not duplicate
record-owned semantics or form a thirteenth Control family.

## Control Record Reference

A typed reference containing one exact Control record kind, identity, positive
revision, and logical revision digest in one bound Control Record Store. It is
resolved against the immutable retained revision, not exported Markdown, a
database row identifier, a path, or whichever revision is latest.

## Control Record Store

The runtime-owned primary SQLite store for one Delivery. It atomically retains
Control revisions, relationships, append-only events, and references to bounded
content-addressed adjacent files. SQL is not an Agent or interface contract.
After Closure is the final Journal head, the runtime seals the logical inventory
and archives the complete store off HEAD. The SQLite file digest is retrieval
integrity, not semantic identity.

## Control Record Store Archive

The complete immutable off-HEAD retrieval unit created from one closed and
sealed Delivery Control Record Store. It contains the SQLite database, every
referenced adjacent file, and one archive manifest binding their exact bytes.
It is not an import format, Product State, canonical repository Control, or a
semantic identity derived from SQLite page layout.

## Control Record Store Seal

The immutable logical-inventory commitment created only when Closure is the
final Journal head, the complete store validates, and every governed draft is
disposed. It binds the exact Closure revision, event head, and logical
inventory. Sealing and archiving are post-Closure runtime boundaries; exact
recovery may finish them without appending another event.

## Document Reference

A typed reference containing an exact document kind, stable identity, and
external whole-document digest. A fragment reference additionally contains one
explicit heading anchor and the digest of the exact addressed fragment. A path
or display title alone is not a Document Reference.

## Description

A governed code-adjacent Knowledge record that owns the responsibility,
behavior, constraints, and rationale of one implementation unit and declares
its exact implementation coverage.

## Delivery

The Lifecycle Process that governs one bounded product result from objective
through accepted canonical change or truthful no-ship. One fresh
`delivery.prepare` creates its sole preparation activity and one primary
Control Record Store; another reconnaissance starts another Delivery.

## Delivery Inbox

A bounded target-scoped runtime read model that enumerates independently
coherent active and archived Deliveries from their exact Control Record Stores.
It is disposable, does not select an ambient Delivery, and cannot merge or move
Process state.

## Delivery View

One disposable coordinate-bound runtime read model for an exact Delivery. It
joins reducer state, current Control subjects, Attempt interpretation, Candidate
condition, Decision Readiness, and eligible operations without becoming a
Control record, event, Process state, or transition permit.

## Evidence

Authenticated observations that can support a proposition about an exact
subject. Evidence does not create product meaning or grant authority.

## Evidence Ledger

One typed normalized collection inside an Evidence Packet covering artifact
facts, Description coverage, Receipt reuse, invalidation, reviewer
independence, proposition decisions, or obligation state. A deterministic
ledger has no independent edit window, authority act, or retained record
identity.

## Evidence Packet

The immutable Control revision that binds one Candidate Revision and Seal,
final Check Receipts, independent reviewer Work Product and Receipt, all
Evidence ledgers, unresolved uncertainty, reviewer identity, and active Work
Boundary.

## Execution Backend

The private Lifecycle Runtime adapter that operates one exact class of
disposable execution environment. It allocates, dispatches, observes, cancels,
retrieves, and reclaims an Execution Cell under a selected Backend Profile. It
cannot select a Delivery operation, advance Process state, create Candidate or
Evidence meaning, authenticate authority, or decide Retirement.

## Execution Backend Profile

The exact installed selection of Backend implementation, Image-selection and
cell-side runner rules, transport, limits, provider control-plane requirements,
Agent-network posture, and observation guarantees for one execution class. The
Specification selects the exact compatible Execution Image. The Profile is
distinct from a Provider Descriptor and Capability Profile and contains no
credential bytes, private endpoint, Docker identifier, Handle, or host path.

## Execution Cell

One bounded disposable Backend allocation for one exact Agent Attempt or
executed Check Specification. It is subordinate mechanism, not a Process,
Delivery operation, Candidate, workspace continuity owner, public resource, or
CLI/TUI abstraction. Provider turns inside one funded Attempt share that
Attempt's Cell; a later Attempt receives a fresh Cell. A Check classified before
allocation creates no Cell.

## Execution Containment

The runtime-established terminal observation that one exact Cell has no
continuing process or writer inside its selected effect boundary. Containment
is required before output retrieval can promote facts and before Retirement.
It does not claim physical deletion of the Backend allocation.

## Execution Handle

An opaque installation-private coordinate for reconciling one exact Cell after
interruption. The Handle is neither an allocation key nor dispatch authority
and never enters Control payloads, Evidence, public runtime protocols, CLI/TUI
input, or Candidate identity.

## Execution Image

The exact digest-identified executable environment selected by an Execution
Backend Profile for one Cell. The Image includes the fixed cell-side runner and
provider or Check tooling selected by the Specification. A mutable tag or
host-installed executable path is not Image identity.

## Execution Input Set

The immutable digest-bound inputs selected for one Cell. Depending on the
owner, they include an exact Candidate Revision Carrier, Projection, Check
material, and bounded Runtime support. Reconnaissance has no Candidate input.
Backend-created materialization paths, credential bytes, and private
coordinates are not Input Set content.

## Execution Observation

A bounded Backend fact about one exact Specification and Cell, including
whether productive execution may have started, current standing, terminal
outcome, and writer or Containment standing. It is independently interpreted
by the Runtime and cannot by itself create a Receipt, Candidate Revision,
Evidence fact, Retirement, or Process transition.

## Execution Output Carrier

The complete bounded provisional output retrieved from one contained Cell. It
is inventoried by an Execution Output Manifest and independently validated by
the Runtime. It is not a Candidate Revision Carrier and cannot promote itself.

## Execution Output Manifest

The digest-bound terminal inventory of one Execution Output Carrier, completed
after all selected output bytes. The Runtime revalidates its completeness,
paths, types, bounds, and byte digests after Containment. Missing, extra,
aliased, special, or mismatched output invalidates the manifest.

## Execution Receipt

A runtime-observed Control revision for one Agent Attempt. It references the
exact Attempt, optional submitted Agent Work Product, exact input and optional
successor Candidate Revision, stable Specification, Input Set, Backend Profile,
Image, output, provider, parser/compiler, Containment, Retirement, and bounded
terminal facts. It does not repeat the full Attempt, expose the private Handle,
embed raw provider bytes, establish a Check proposition, report mutable
Reclamation progress, or decide progress.

## Execution Reclamation

The installation-private maintenance transition that establishes physical
absence of one exact retired Backend allocation. Reclamation may finish after
Activity completion and Closure. Failure leaves inert observable residue and a
durable obligation; it does not restore dispatch authority or change Process
truth.

## Execution Retirement

The Runtime-owned durable decision that one exact Handle can never again be
productively dispatched or reused, output disposition is final, and any
remaining physical allocation has a tracked Reclamation owner. Retirement is a
required correctness boundary; it is not a Backend method or proof of physical
absence.

## Execution Specification

The immutable Runtime-issued instruction for one exact Agent Attempt or Check
execution. It binds the Activity and owner subject, Backend Profile, Image,
runner, Input Set, capability, effective limits, and output policy. A private
unpredictable allocation key is separately generated and durably bound to it;
reusing that exact pair is deterministic, but the key is not a public digest or
part of Specification identity.

## Founder

The person or configured principal retaining final product judgment and the
authority to authenticate designated Process transitions. An interface can act
for the Founder only through explicit, scoped authorization.

## Founder Brief

One complete fresh Founder-supplied semantic Markdown input finalized before
an exact agent activity starts. Preparation, continuation, evaluation,
revision, and reaffirmation each use the Brief profile owned by their operation.
The bound Agent Attempt cannot inherit or merge another Brief, Work Product,
interface transcript, or provider conversation.

## Frame

The founder-facing presentation for starting a fresh reconnaissance Delivery.
Frame accepts one complete fresh Brief, presents the resulting Work Boundary
revision and exact dossier, and hands its Delivery identity to explicit
admission. A new Frame submission creates a fresh Delivery and does not inherit
another Brief or provider conversation. Frame is not Process state, Control,
or an authority route.

## Fresh-Project Cut

A hard cut whose successor support begins only with newly initialized successor
repositories. It has no predecessor semantic or operational reader, import,
adoption, migration, migration rollback, active-attempt continuation, or
mixed-generation mode. This excludes a rollback from successor state to
predecessor meaning. It does not prohibit exact rollback of initializer-owned
effects after a failed
fresh initialization transaction. Discriminator-only inspection required to
reject unsupported input is not a compatibility reader.

## Implementation Artifact

Executable code, test code, configuration, documentation, or another product
file. It is repository reality rather than a governed Knowledge record. A
Description can own its semantic coverage.

## Investment

The caller-funded reasoning resources allocated to one Agent Attempt, such as
model class, reasoning effort, wall time, and cumulative policy limits.
Investment changes neither scope nor authority.

## Knowledge

Durable repository-addressable material that can govern or explain future
work. Lifecycle governs Behavior, Assurance, Blueprint, Description, and Check
Definition records.

## Knowledge Closure

The complete set of Knowledge records and relationships required to interpret
and validate one Work Boundary for one role.

## Knowledge Projection

A deterministic immutable compilation of the exact Knowledge Closure required
by one role, plus provenance, conflicts, unresolved references, reachable
context, and an omission manifest.

## Knowledge Set

The complete validated graph of governed Knowledge at one repository state.

## Lifecycle Document

Strict UTF-8 Markdown framed by exact `---` delimiter lines around one RFC 8259
JSON header. The header owns typed operational values; the CommonMark body owns
readable semantics and cannot override the header. Runtime-generated Lifecycle
Documents use one canonical renderer and are identified by an external
whole-document digest rather than a self-digest field. Knowledge uses this
form directly. Control inspection and export can render one exact retained
revision into this form without becoming a duplicate primary store.

## Lifecycle Runtime

The trusted product actor that owns Delivery Control, Candidate Revision
lineage and Carriers, Evidence compilation, authority verification, Backend
selection, and exact Process transitions. It delegates bounded physical
execution to subordinate Backends but does not delegate product or Process
authority. A later Runtime Image or Launcher is distribution machinery, not a
second controller or current Foundation requirement.

## Material Condition

A fact that prevents honest continuation under the active Work Boundary without
founder judgment, mandate revision, capability change, or no-ship. It is not a
local implementation defect. Delivery freezes the exact condition and its
source as an immutable retained Control revision before productive continuation
is blocked.

## No-ship

A terminal Delivery disposition establishing that the candidate was not made
canonical. It can also truthfully close an unsuccessful fresh preparation
before a Work Boundary or Candidate exists; in that case the Decision and
Closure omit both subjects. No-ship is an authoritative result, not an
unrecorded failure or an agent summary.

## Obligation

One enumerated condition that an accepted result must satisfy. Obligations can
originate in Behavior, Assurance, Blueprint, Description, effects, risks,
artifacts, Checks, exclusions, or acceptance propositions.

## Process

A stateful contract governing one class of truth transition, including its
admission subject, reversible work object, evidence, authority, exceptional
paths, terminal dispositions, and information closure.

## Store Journal

The closed twenty-two-kind append-only Control Record Event sequence inside one
Control Record Store. Deterministic reduction over the complete validated chain
and exact referenced revisions reproduces Process state. Ordinary edits,
commands, provider streams, compiler microsteps, and model reasoning are not
Store Journal events.

## Delivery State View

The ephemeral in-memory Delivery view returned by reducing one complete valid
event sequence. It separates Delivery standing, active activities, recovery
obligations, Candidate condition, current subject revisions, Journal head, and
eligible operations. Those dimensions are orthogonal: Boundary governance does
not imply Candidate existence, activity or Store recovery does not invent or
replace Candidate condition, every unresolved Activity can be recoverable while
pre-effect Candidate work remains in progress, and Closure remains terminal
while Store disposition completes. It is not a retained Control revision,
mutable state row, or transition authority.

## Product State

The exact repository-native state that participates in product meaning and
behavior. Product State includes governed Knowledge, Atlas inputs bound by the
Process, implementation artifacts, and other declared product sources. It
excludes compatible process-only Control and ignored runtime support. A
Product State digest identifies one complete repository epoch. During an
admitted Delivery, the active branch lease treats any canonical or
authoritative-worktree change as a violation; Atlas receives no movement
exception.

## Publication Manifest

The canonical self-digesting inventory of one exact Lifecycle specification
revision's documents, schemas, fixtures, file-byte digests, and common document
maturity. It does not carry publication lifecycle status.

## Publication Statement

A separately authenticated carrier establishing `draft`, `candidate`,
`released`, `superseded`, or `withdrawn` lifecycle status for one exact
Publication Manifest and Release Notes digest. Its signing authority is
distinct from target-repository Founder authority and conformance-claim
issuance.

## Projection Compiler

A conforming implementation that validates a Knowledge Set and deterministically
produces a role-specific Knowledge Projection for an exact Work Boundary and
repository state.

## Provider Adapter

The narrow implementation translating an Agent Attempt to and from a concrete
agent host, such as `codex exec`. It does not own product or Process semantics.

## Provider Descriptor

An exact installed carrier describing one Provider Adapter's implementation,
supported provider host inside an Execution Cell, governed workspace access,
cell-side terminal observation, semantic compilation, product-capability
support, and provider control-plane service and credential-class requirements.
It does not grant Backend authority or declare provider-authored structured
output or terminal text as a Control contract.

## Proposition

One exact claim that independent review must judge about the sealed candidate.
A proposition declares its required evidence and any Behavior, Assurance,
effect, risk, artifact, or Check coverage.

## Receipt

See Check Receipt.

## Release Notes

The immutable content-addressed sidecar describing every normative change,
compatibility and security effect, known limitation, operator action, and
hard-cut treatment for one exact Publication Manifest. It is authenticated by
the Publication Statement and is not inventoried by the manifest it names.

## Repository Snapshot

One digest-bound repository epoch composed from target identity, commit, tree,
Git object format, repository contract, Product State, Atlas State, complete
valid Atlas Resolution, normalized Atlas model, and one already-validated
Knowledge Set.

## Role

The bounded job an Agent performs in an Agent Attempt. Standard Delivery roles
are reconnaissance, builder, and reviewer.

## Role Brief

The exact runtime-authored provider-visible assignment that quotes the
normalized fresh Founder direction and presents the role, subject, Work
Boundary, Projection, capability, governed semantic file, body template,
submission rule, and authority limits. It exposes no Control envelope and
cannot widen the typed operation or authority. It is input, not a provider
proposal.

## Controlled Authoring Workspace

One Attempt-scoped area inside an Execution Cell containing the sole
Agent-editable semantic Markdown file. Only the assigned Agent can change it
during the policy-defined window. It has no Process standing and is not
authoritative until the runtime contains the Cell, retrieves the output, and
independently observes, validates, and compiles one exact revision. The Agent
can edit the same file across provider turns in that funded Attempt.
Intermediate edits are not Journal events, and another Attempt reconstructs a
fresh workspace rather than continuing this path. Foundation rc.10 exposes no
provider-invoked live semantic validator; only post-Containment Runtime
validation can establish whether the final draft is valid.

## Agent Work Product Compiler

The deterministic runtime component that accepts normalized semantic
intermediate representation and exact submitted Markdown, injects fixed
Attempt, role, and subject bindings, resolves references and global identities,
normalizes set-like values, renders canonical semantic Markdown, validates the
complete revision, and computes its logical digest. A compiler invariant
failure is a runtime failure, not an invalid Agent result.

## Agent Work Product Projection Citation

Agent-proposed claim support that names one exact unambiguous mounted reachable
item from the Attempt-bound Knowledge Projection. It does not make the item
Knowledge, source context, Candidate material, Evidence, boundary authority, or
a selectable Work Boundary source.

## Semantic Submission

The exact body-only Markdown observed from a governed working document at its
submission boundary. It contains claims, decisions, uncertainty, citations,
and proposed effects using Attempt-local handles. It contains no SQL, front
matter, wrapper, fixed Attempt bindings, global identities, digests, ordering
assertions, or runtime facts. Strict parsing produces a normalized runtime
value or classifies the submission as invalid. Its observed byte digest is
provenance and is distinct from the body digest of the runtime-canonical
Markdown retained in the Agent Work Product.

## Post-Containment Semantic Validation

The mandatory Runtime operation that reopens the exact final governed Agent
draft from an independently validated Output Carrier only after Execution
Containment, then applies the parser and compiler basis selected by the frozen
Attempt. It can retain one valid Agent Work Product or one bounded final
submission diagnostic. Foundation rc.10 has no provider-invoked live semantic
validator or Cell validity claim.

## Semantic Operation Input

The bounded UTF-8 Markdown through which the Founder supplies only the meaning
required by one public v10 Delivery operation. Preparation carries one complete,
self-contained reconnaissance brief for a fresh Delivery; it does not inherit
a prior Brief, Boundary, TUI transcript, or provider conversation.
Continuation and evaluation carry role direction; revision and reaffirmation
carry exact resolution rationale; and no-ship carries an exact reason plus
explicit Candidate disposition. Runtime bindings, Investment mechanics,
machine configuration, authority bytes, state projections, package identities,
digests, timestamps, and operation eligibility are derived from their owners.

## Sealed Candidate

The Candidate while its exact current state is bound by the current Candidate
Seal for evaluation. This is a lifecycle condition, not a separate product
object, record family, or identity. If correction resumes, the prior Seal
remains immutable but no longer describes the Candidate's current state; a
later evaluation must create a new Seal.

## Semantic Thin Waist

The stable identities, relationship types, authority classes, and falsifiable
obligations connecting broad project context to implementation.

## Source Material

Rationale, research, evidence, architecture, implementation, history, or other
material retained by an exact source owner and reached through a Knowledge
source or an Atlas Content or typed Reference edge. Source material can inform
Knowledge and Agent judgment but does not silently become Product meaning,
Process Control, Evidence, or authority.

## Source Revision

The immutable repository commit, content digest, or external immutable identity
that fixes the bytes of one Knowledge source used by a Knowledge Set or
Projection.

## Specification Publication Authority

The independently trusted authority class that signs Publication Statement
subjects. It can authenticate publication lifecycle status but cannot make a
Draft document Accepted or establish implementation conformance.

## Supersession

An explicit relationship by which a newer Knowledge record replaces an older
record's current authority. Historical records remain addressable but cannot be
silently selected as current.

## Truth Transition

An authorized state change that makes a proposal, record, or product result
canonical for its authority class.

## Work Boundary

Delivery's immutable proposed or active mandate and final comparison basis. It
binds the selected result, knowledge, obligations, exclusions, effects, risks,
artifacts, Checks, acceptance propositions, capability, and exact bases.
Required baseline Receipts bind this exact Boundary and determine its proposal
readiness under their selected temporal modalities; they are not fields or
forward references inside it. The runtime compiles one complete immutable
revision from a Founder Brief, reconnaissance Work Product, and runtime facts,
then executes its baseline Checks. It is admission-ready only when the reducer
observes the complete modality-valid Receipt set, and becomes active only when
an applied Founder Admission Decision selects those same Boundary bytes. Its
active standing begins independently of the later initial Candidate
observation.
Proposed, admission-ready, and active are derived standings, not separate
record kinds.

## Work Boundary Revision

One immutable version of the Work Boundary. Revision 1 is the initial complete
mandate revision and becomes proposed only after its modality-valid baseline
Receipt set is complete. A later revision references its active predecessor
and, when applicable, the exact Material Condition and Founder resolution
rationale. Historical revisions remain addressable. Admission selects a
revision without copying it into an admitted carrier.

## Founder Decision

One runtime-constructed, Founder-authenticated Control revision with a typed
variant: admit, readmit, accept, or no-ship. It references the exact Boundary,
Condition, Candidate Revision, Seal, Evidence Packet, and disposition required
by that variant. Admission also references every required modality-valid
baseline Receipt; readmission distinguishes the selected successor Boundary
from its active predecessor. Authentication freezes the Decision; a separate
transaction intent and truthful observation determine whether its selected
effect became Process or product truth.

## Decision Readiness

A non-authoritative section of one current Delivery View that joins the exact
Boundary, Candidate, Seal, Evidence, eligible operation, and any already
authenticated Founder Decision needed for review. It cannot authenticate,
authorize, or apply a transition.

## Working Candidate

The Candidate while productive attempts or bounded correction remain possible
under the active Work Boundary. This is a lifecycle condition of the same
Candidate, not a separate product object, record family, or identity.
