# Clarity Intake

## Purpose

Use Clarity Intake to decide what Context pressure the run owns.

The intake output is an active Clarity Boundary. It should make the Context
scope smaller than the whole product unless the whole product route is the
actual work.

## Questions

Answer:

```text
What Context area or cluster is under pressure?
Why now?
What product question should become easier to answer?
What review depth is required?
What Context mutations are allowed?
What surfaces are explicitly excluded?
What should remain only a recommendation?
```

## Exit

Exit when the Clarity Boundary can control the pass without reading the whole
corpus again.
