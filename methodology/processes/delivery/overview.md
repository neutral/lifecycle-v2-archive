# Delivery

## Purpose

Use Delivery to turn one selected Signal into bounded production change through
explicit product judgment, active admission, and current proof.

Delivery starts from a concrete Signal. The Signal may come directly from the
founder-dev, runtime observation, support issue, or Discovery selection. A
Signal is not itself permission to build. Framing weighs semantic authority,
discipline, repo facts, risk, tradeoffs, exclusions, and falsifiers into
the product judgment recorded by the active Work Boundary.

Use [../discovery/process.md](../discovery/process.md) when the next useful
work is unclear.

## Process Boundary

Delivery admits, builds, proves, reconciles, lands, releases when applicable,
classifies runtime and learning, and closes one selected Signal.

Delivery does not require Discovery history. When Discovery happened, Delivery
uses the selected Signal as input and may use the selection handoff as
admission context.

Delivery does require an admitted product judgment before Build changes
production behavior. That judgment remains process-local Control state unless
durable learning is promoted into semantic authority during closure.
